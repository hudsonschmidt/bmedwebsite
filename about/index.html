About

Every forecasting season came with the inevitable question: “Will I survive this this course load?” During my first few years at Cal Poly, staring down another quarter of biomedical engineering courses combined with my pre-med classes, I’d be lying if I said I didn’t quake in my boots… just a little.

So what did I do? I lived up to the detective in my namesake and searched for answers from multiple inputs. I talked to peers, upperclassmen, faculty, and Cal Poly advisors. Although every conversation gave me another piece of the puzzle, no one person I found seemed to be walking the same path I was. It felt like I was charting fresh steps in a snowstorm trying to find my way out of half-covered tracks.

But it doesn’t have to feel that way; the truth is there is a small but mighty group of us non-conventional pre-med majors who get it. We have the power to encourage and advise each other, but the true challenge is finding each other in this blizzard. This is the drive that has prompted us to form the Biomedical Engineering pre-medical club or BPM as we call it.

Goal: BPM exists to support engineering students on the path to medicine. We are building a unified community where pre-medical engineers find peers who understand this path, and provide the insight students need to make informed career decisions. Our members work to understand the medical profession while recognizing the distinct perspective an engineering background brings to it — all in pursuit of a better future for the health of humanity, and with the utmost respect for all people, regardless of identity, gender, sexuality, race, culture, age, or disability.
